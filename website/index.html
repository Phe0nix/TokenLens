FAQ

Questions teams ask before rollout

What is included in Free?

Free is for evaluation and light usage: up to 10 scans per month, 3 snapshots, core exports, inspect mode, layout tools, and color blindness preview.

What does Pro unlock?

Pro removes scan and snapshot limits and adds the full export family, audit reports, insights, theme personalization, Smart Apply beta, and production workflow utilities.

Is the $50 price monthly or yearly?

The annual option is $50 per year. The pricing card can also show a monthly view for comparison.

Is Palext only for designers?

No. It is built for both designers and developers with shared language and actionable export outputs.

Can we use it for accessibility triage?

Yes. Palext highlights contrast risk, live AA failures, and provides clear fix-focused sections in reports.
