Overview

Palext keeps data local to your browser

Palext extracts design tokens from websites you visit, but it does not send that information to any server. The extension operates locally in your browser and stores only the data needed to make the tool useful.

Storage

What is saved

Palext uses chrome.storage.local to save snapshots, export preferences, UI mode, and temporary inspect state. You can clear saved data from the extension itself whenever you want.

Saves snapshots, preferences, and history locally.

Never

What Palext never does

No analytics

Palext does not run tracking or telemetry.
